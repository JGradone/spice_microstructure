VMP5500 cast comparisons

Every VMP5500 cast against the nearest ship CTD cast and the nearest VMP250 profile reaching 100 m, if one is within 15 km and 12 h. Depth profiles show heave between casts; the T–S panel does not, so judge salinity there. VMP5500 CT data sit at the CT sensor's depth, 1.57 m above the pressure sensor. Noise and offset are measured over 150–500 m. The aqua series corrects for the Sea-Bird response lag: temperature is the nose FP07 moved to the CT sensor, and salinity uses the measured conductivity with that temperature slowed to match the SBE4 cell (time constant fitted per cast, about 6 s). This removes most of the salty offset; the scatter that remains is the unexplained noise, largest at staircase interfaces.
