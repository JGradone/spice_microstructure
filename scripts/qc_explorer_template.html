What the QC choices change

From the baseline run (both instruments, all casts). Each finding can be reproduced with the controls.

Stratification moves the ratio far more than any epsilon QC choice

perturb's window-scale N² for the VMP5500 is about 4.5× larger, and its dT/dz about 2.3× smaller, than TEOS-10 values from the same instrument's CT and SA (SPC_033, 150–250 m: N² 2.7×10⁻⁴ vs 6.0×10⁻⁵ s⁻²). The VMP250's window values agree with TEOS-10. The shipboard archive has the same values, so shipboard VMP5500 ratios inherit the error. TEOS-10 N² from the VMP5500 is itself negative in 34–44% of windows at 150–250 m because its salinity is spiky. Switch the stratification control to see the ratio change by an order of magnitude; the hydrography step has to fix this before any ratio is interpreted.

The two instruments agree where both measure

At the process station between 150 and 250 m, median ε is 8.9×10⁻¹⁰ (VMP250 free fall) and 8.0×10⁻¹⁰ W kg⁻¹ (VMP5500); median χ is 3.9×10⁻⁸ and 3.7×10⁻⁸ K² s⁻¹. Epsilon and chi are not the source of the instruments' different ratios.

Their noise floors differ by a factor of ten

The lowest epsilon each instrument reports (0.5–2nd percentile below 50 m) is about 3×10⁻¹⁰ for the VMP250 and 3×10⁻¹¹ W kg⁻¹ for the VMP5500. Below 150 m the VMP250 median sits within about 2× of its floor, so VMP250 epsilon there is close to an upper bound, which makes χ expected an upper bound and the ratio a lower bound. Excluding below-floor windows raises the VMP5500 mean by 24%, a bias; setting them to the floor changes it little.

The ATOMIX figure-of-merit cut removes half the data at every level

FM above 1.15 on one or both probes removes about 36% of VMP250 and 54% of VMP5500 windows beyond segment QC, and the rejected fraction is 41–77% in every epsilon decade, including high epsilon. That pattern points to the threshold not suiting these spectra (4 s FFT, 16 s windows) rather than to bad data. Inspect spectra before adopting it; it lowers the 150–600 m mean ε by 32% (VMP250) and 13% (VMP5500).

Segment QC matters little below 50 m

The proposed thresholds remove 4.4% of VMP250 and 2.3% of VMP5500 windows, mostly in the top 20 m, and change the 150–600 m mean ε by less than 3%. Variance resolved never binds (under 0.1% of windows below 0.5), and the probe-pair rule changes means by under 1%.
