VMP5500 conductivity: salinity is noisy, biased salty, and worsens through the cruise

Bottom line. Across {{n:profiles.vmp5500}} VMP5500 casts, salinity from the SBE4 is about {{n:noise_ratio}}× noisier than the VMP250's at 1 m scale ({{n:noise_median.vmp5500}} vs {{n:noise_median.vmp250}} g/kg) and reads +{{n:offset_median.vmp5500}} g/kg salty of the ship CTD (median; the VMP250 is +{{n:offset_median.vmp250}}). Both get worse after about 24 August.

The error is in the conductivity channel. A conductivity–temperature timing mismatch, the calibration coefficients and the temperature channel's sampling have been checked and ruled out. Physical trouble with the cell during the deployment fits best, but the salty sign is unusual for fouling, and we would like a second opinion.

How the comparison works

A ship CTD cast lies within 15 km and 6 h of 42 of the 45 VMP5500 casts. Casts hours apart see the thermocline heaved up and down by internal waves, so comparing at the same depth mixes real displacement into the difference. The quantitative comparisons are therefore made along the temperature–salinity curve: VMP salinity is compared with the ship CTD's salinity at the same conservative temperature Θ, which heave does not change.

Questions for you

  1. Does a salty bias that grows over a few weeks, with growing small-scale noise, match anything you have seen from an unpumped SBE4 on a free-falling profiler? We expected fouling to bias it fresh.
  2. Could anything mechanical (a crack, debris or sargassum in the cell, a connector) produce this pattern?
  3. Would you recommend a post-cruise calibration of SBE4 #4076, or another test, to confirm before we write it off?

Method notes

  • Profiles processed with microstructure-tpw; salinity and Θ with TEOS-10 (gsw). VMP data binned to 1 m in depth.
  • Noise: standard deviation of 1 m salinity minus its 9 m running median, 150–500 m. Offset: median of VMP salinity minus ship CTD salinity at the same Θ, using the ship cast's T–S relation (median SA in 0.05 °C bins).
  • Ship CTD: SBE 911, sensor pair 1, nearest cast within 15 km and 6 h. N²: TEOS-10 from linear fits of Θ and SA over the stated scale.
  • VMP250: {{n:profiles.vmp250}} casts reaching 200 m. The figures and numbers come from scripts/diagnose_vmp5500_conductivity.py in the processing repository.
